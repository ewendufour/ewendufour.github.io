Goal

Build the four foundations at the top right, one per suit, from Ace to King.

How to play

  • In the seven columns, stack cards in descending order, alternating red and black.
  • A run already in order moves as a block.
  • A face-down card is revealed as soon as it reaches the top of its column.
  • Only a King, or a run starting with a King, can go on an empty column.
  • Tapping the stock turns 1 or 3 cards onto the waste, and only the last one is playable.
  • Once the stock is empty, tapping it puts the waste back, with no limit on passes.
  • A card can come back down from a foundation to a column.

Not every deal can be won, especially when drawing 3 cards.

Controls

  • Drag a card or a run, or tap it and then tap its destination.
  • A double click sends the card to the best available spot.
  • "Auto-move" sends every card it can to the foundations.
  • "Undo" takes back one move, as many times as you like.
