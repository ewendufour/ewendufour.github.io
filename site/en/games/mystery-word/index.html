Goal

Find the hidden word. You get one more guess than the word has letters: 6 guesses for 5 letters, 8 for 7.

After each guess

  • Filled square: the letter is in the right spot.
  • Thick border: the letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
  • Dashed border: the letter is not in the word, or not any further times.

A repeated letter is only flagged as many times as it appears in the word.

A guess that is not in the dictionary is refused and does not count. The dictionary includes plurals and verb forms, but it can miss a rare word.

The word to find is always a common one. Dictionary: an-array-of-english-words (MIT licence).

Controls

Use the on-screen keyboard or your own: Enter submits and Backspace deletes.
