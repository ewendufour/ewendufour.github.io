Goal

Line up four of your pieces, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, before the computer does.

How to play

  • Players take turns choosing a column: the piece falls to the lowest free cell.
  • If the board fills up with no line, the game is a draw.
  • The first player changes every game.

Difficulty

  • Easy: the computer plays at random, but never misses an immediate win.
  • Medium: it blocks you and looks a few moves ahead.
  • Hard: it calculates seven moves ahead.

Controls

Click a column, or use keys 1 to 7.
