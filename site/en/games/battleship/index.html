      Goal

      Sink the computer's five ships before it sinks yours.

      Placement

      • Each side places a carrier (5 squares), a battleship (4), a cruiser (3), a submarine (3) and a destroyer (2).
      • Tap a square to place the ship shown. "Horizontal" or the R key changes its direction.
      • Tap a ship already placed to pick it back up.
      • Ships cannot overlap, but they can touch.

      Battle

      • Each side fires once per turn, even after a hit. The first player changes every game.
      • A dot marks a miss, a cross a hit, and a filled square a sunk ship.
      • When a ship sinks, its name and squares are announced to both sides.

      The computer does not cheat

      It only knows the answers to its own shots: miss, hit or sunk. From those it works out where your ships can still be and keeps firing at a damaged ship, without ever seeing your grid. At the end, its remaining ships are revealed.

      Keyboard

      Arrow keys move around a grid, and Enter places a ship or fires.
