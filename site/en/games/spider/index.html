Goal

Build eight complete runs of one suit, from King to Ace. Each one leaves the game by itself.

How to play

  • A card goes on a card one rank higher, whatever its suit.
  • Only a descending run of a single suit moves as a block.
  • Any card or run can go on an empty column.
  • A face-down card is revealed as soon as it reaches the top of its column.
  • Tapping the stock deals one card onto every column, provided none is empty.

The number of suits sets the difficulty: 1 is the easiest, 4 the hardest. Not every deal can be won.

Controls

  • Drag a card or a run, or tap it and then tap its destination.
  • A double click sends the card to the best available spot.
  • "Undo" takes back one move, as many times as you like.
