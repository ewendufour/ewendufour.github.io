Goal

Line up three Xs in a row, column or diagonal before the computer lines up three Os.

How to play

  • Players take turns placing their symbol on a free square.
  • If the board fills up with no line, the game is a draw.
  • The first player changes every game.

Difficulty

  • Easy: the computer plays at random.
  • Medium: it wins or blocks you when it can, and plays at random otherwise.
  • Hard: it plays perfectly, so the best you can get is a draw.
