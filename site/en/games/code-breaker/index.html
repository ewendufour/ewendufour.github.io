Goal

Find the secret combination drawn by the computer, in at most ten guesses. A colour can appear several times in the code.

After each guess

  • Solid dot: a peg has the right colour in the right place.
  • Hollow dot: a peg has the right colour in the wrong place.

The dots do not say which pegs they refer to. A repeated peg is only counted as many times as it is in the code.

Difficulty

Choose 4 or 5 positions, and 6 or 8 colours. Changing a setting starts a new game.

Controls

Tap a colour to fill the row, then submit. On a keyboard, digits pick a colour, Backspace deletes and Enter submits.
