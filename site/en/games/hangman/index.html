Goal

Find the hidden word before making seven mistakes.

How to play

  • Suggest a letter with the on-screen keyboard or your own.
  • If it is in the word, it appears in all its positions.
  • If not, a stroke is added to the drawing. At the seventh, you lose and the word is revealed.

Words have 5 to 9 letters. A letter already played does not count twice.
