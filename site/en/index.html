About

I work on ways to compute efficiently in homotopy type theory and in cubical type theory.

My PhD thesis, « Calculer en théorie homotopique des types » (Computing in Homotopy Type Theory), is co-supervised by Viktoria Heu and Nicolas Magaud, and co-advised by Loïc Pujet and Pierre Boutry.

Research internships

  1. 2026 Canonical for (Cubical) Agda, ICube, with Pierre Boutry, Chase Norman and Loïc Pujet. Report (in French)
  2. 2025 Measuring space complexity in the lambda calculus, LMF, with Thibaut Balabonski. Report (in French)
  3. 2024 Deductive verification of assembly programs with Why3, LMF, with Jean-Christophe Filliâtre and Paul Patault. Report (in French)
